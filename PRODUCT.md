## Users

Residents and businesses in Pato Branco - PR (and nearby) who want flowers, plants, or landscaping — people buying a bouquet for an occasion (Valentine's Day, Mother's Day, birthdays), decorating a home/office with plants and pots, or hiring garden design/maintenance. They land on the site from an Instagram bio link or WhatsApp referral and need to see the product quality fast, then order via WhatsApp or find the store.

## Product Purpose

Marketing/ordering landing page for Flora Garden, a real flower shop + plant nursery + landscaping studio ("Floricultura e Paisagismo") in Pato Branco, PR, Brazil. Purpose: convert visitors into WhatsApp orders/inquiries and store visits, and present the brand as polished as its Instagram presence.

## Positioning

Not just a flower delivery order form — Flora Garden covers three connected offerings under one roof: handmade bouquets/arrangements, plants + pots + substrates for the home, and full landscaping design/execution/maintenance for residential and corporate gardens. The mechanism a pure flower-delivery competitor can't copy: walk-in store you can browse, backed by a landscaping team for bigger projects.
